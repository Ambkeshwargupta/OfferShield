# Privacy

OfferShield is designed to minimise the information retained during offer investigation.

- Do not paste Aadhaar, PAN, bank account details, full home addresses, signatures, or other unnecessary personal information.
- The initial app accepts text for analysis; original PDFs and email files are not uploaded.
- The analysis engine extracts only practical indicators such as email domains, URLs, telephone numbers, claimed company names, identifiers, role clues and payment-related phrases.
- Saved reports contain extracted indicators and findings, not the raw message text.
- Reports are linked to a random browser session. Clearing browser cookies will make the saved reports inaccessible from that browser.
- Use the delete action to remove an individual report.
- This prototype does not provide registered user accounts, cross-device syncing or a formal data-subject request workflow.

This page is product documentation, not a legal privacy notice. Review applicable Indian data-protection obligations before public production launch.
